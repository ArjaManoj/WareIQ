# WareIQ Official Website Analysis & Business Domain Modeling

## 1. Executive Summary & Company Positioning
- **Company**: WareIQ (https://wareiq.com/)
- **Core Positioning**: India's leading eCommerce fulfillment and modern supply chain tech platform providing Amazon-grade Same-Day & Next-Day fulfillment for high-growth D2C, B2B, and Omnichannel brands.
- **Key Proposition**:
  - Pan-India distributed fulfillment network (Tier 1 & Tier 2 hubs).
  - Smart Inventory Placement (SIP) powered by machine learning.
  - Multi-courier aggregation with automated courier allocation.
  - Non-Delivery Report (NDR) automation & RTO (Return to Origin) reduction suite.
  - Seller of Record (SOR) multi-state tax compliance enablement.
  - Enterprise control tower and post-purchase customer engagement tools.

## 2. Core Business Solutions & Capabilities
| Solution | Official Description & Capabilities | Target Segment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D2C Fulfillment** | Nationwide distributed warehousing, automated pick-pack-ship, same-day dispatch cutoffs (till 4 PM), real-time inventory sync across Shopify, WooCommerce, Magento. | Direct-to-Consumer digital-first brands |
| **Marketplace Fulfillment** | Multi-marketplace compliance (Amazon FBA/Seller Flex, Flipkart, Myntra, Nykaa, Tata CLiQ), scheduled appointments, barcoding and palletized inbound/outbound. | Omnichannel brand merchants |
| **Quick Commerce Fulfillment** | Rapid replenishment to dark stores (Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart, BB Now), low-latency staging, micro-SLAs. | Fast-moving consumer brands |
| **B2B / Retail Fulfillment** | Bulk B2B order staging, GT/MT store deliveries, customized ASN/e-way bills, multi-tier pallet logistics. | Wholesale, retail chain suppliers |
| **WareIQ Shipping & Logistics** | Multi-carrier courier aggregator (BlueDart, Delhivery, Xpressbees, DTDC, Shadowfax, Ekart), dynamic routing engine, uniform shipping rates. | All eCommerce shippers |
| **Seller of Record (SOR)** | Complete compliance infrastructure allowing brands to store stock in 10+ states without individual physical branch registration overhead. | Brands scaling nationally |

## 3. Public Industry Verticals
1. **Beauty, Cosmetics & Personal Care**: Batch/expiry tracking, temperature-monitored storage, secure unboxing experience.
2. **Fashion & Apparel**: High SKU counts, size variants, rapid turnaround on returns/reverse QC.
3. **Consumer Electronics & Accessories**: High-value serial tracking, tamper-proof packaging, fragile handling.
4. **Health, Wellness & Nutraceuticals**: Strict FIFO/FEFO dispatching, climate protection, regulatory certification adherence.
5. **Home & Living / Kitchenware**: Heavy & bulky handling, protective bubble packaging, multi-piece box handling.
6. **Sports, Fitness, Luggage & Travel**: Bulky dimension management, volumetric rate optimization.
